Ledger

Capture a payment and the rules engine validates it, calculates the fee and scores risk. Scores of 70 or more go to the review queue.

New payment

Refund a payment

Settled card and wallet payments only; bank transfers cannot be refunded (RTP is irrevocable). Analysts and managers only.

Review queue

Held payments wait here, oldest first. Ask the AI copilot for a recommendation; you make the decision. Chargebacks always come here, to be accepted or contested.

AI triage agent

The agent works through every held payment on its own: for each one it calls the ledger tools (payment, customer history, risk rules, and the dispute playbook for chargebacks) and leaves a recommendation. It never approves or rejects; an analyst decides each payment.

      Escalated cases

        Ops chat

        Ask about PayOps rules or today’s payments. The assistant looks up the ledger with its tools and calculates fees and risk with the rules engine, so every number comes from the app.

        Audit log

        Every user action and every AI recommendation, newest first.

        Self-test

        Runs every automated check on the rules engine, refunds, chargebacks, roles, Reopen and the duplicate guard, and compares each result with its expected answer. Your data is saved first and put back afterwards. AI features are tested live and are not part of this run.

        Click Run all tests. It takes about a second.

        AI evaluation

        Asks the Ops chat agent the 12 HW8 test questions and scores each answer against the known correct value. Results are saved so the team can compare runs.

        Click Run AI evaluation. Each question takes a few seconds.

          Team and roles

          Who can do what. Ops staff capture payments and log disputes; analysts decide held payments, refunds, chargebacks and reopens; managers can do everything and manage roles. Nobody can approve a payment they captured themselves (maker-checker).
